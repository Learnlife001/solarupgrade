Importing directly from Germany

Who actually exports to Nigeria, and what it involves.

What direct import actually involves

Minimum orders

Most German wholesalers sell by the pallet, not the panel. A pallet is roughly 30 panels; a container is far more.

Freight

Sea freight to Lagos takes four to eight weeks. Air freight is faster and rarely worth it on something as heavy as glass.

Duty and clearing

Import duty, VAT and a clearing agent at the port. These are real costs and they land after you have already paid the supplier.

Paying in euro

German suppliers invoice in euro. You carry the exchange rate and the transfer fees, usually before anything ships.

Warranty across borders

A panel that fails in year three has to go back to Germany, or be argued about by email. Ask before you buy, not after.

English is not a given

Plenty of these companies work in German only. Where we know an English email gets answered, the entry says so.

None of that makes direct import a bad idea — for a large order it can be much cheaper. It makes it a project. If you would rather someone else did it, that is what our catalogue is for: naira prices, delivery included, no customs to clear.
