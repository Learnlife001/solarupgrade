What we hold, and why

WhatWhy
Your name and email address To create your account, confirm it is yours, and email you about your orders
Your password To let you sign in. Stored only as a bcrypt hash — we cannot read it, and neither can anyone who steals the database

Who else sees it

Only the companies that make the shop work, and only the part each needs:

  • Our payment providers — the amount and order reference, so they can take payment. Your card details go to them directly and never through us.
  • Our email provider — your email address and the message, so it can be delivered.
  • Our hosting and database providers — they store the data on our behalf and do not use it for anything else.
  • A courier — your name, address and phone number, to deliver your order.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising.

Where it is held

Our database and servers are hosted outside Nigeria, in the European Union. Data moved abroad is protected in transit and at rest by the providers we use.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept while they may be needed for warranty, tax and accounting purposes. Ask us to close your account and we will delete what we are not required to keep, and tell you what remains and why.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. Email [support email address] and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Cookies

One cookie, and it is the one that keeps you signed in. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking on this site, so there is nothing here to consent to or opt out of.
