Returns

Two different things are covered here, and they work differently: changing your mind, and something being wrong.

If you change your mind

You have 7 days from delivery to tell us you want to return an item you simply do not want. It needs to be unused and in its original packaging — solar equipment that has been wired in cannot be resold.

Email [support email address] with your order number before sending anything back, so we can tell you where to send it. Return postage for a change of mind is yours to pay; panels and batteries are heavy, so ask us for a quote first rather than being surprised by it.

We refund within 14 days of the item reaching us, to the method you paid with. Where you paid in a converted currency, we refund the amount you were charged in that currency.

If something is wrong

Faulty, damaged in transit, or not what was described — that is not a change of mind and none of the above applies. We pay the return cost and you get a repair, a replacement or a refund. Tell us within a reasonable time of noticing, and send a photograph if the damage is visible; it usually saves a collection.

Your rights under the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018 sit above this policy and are not limited by it.

What cannot be returned for a change of mind

  • Items that have been installed, wired in or mounted
  • Items made or ordered specifically for you
  • Anything returned without its original packaging

None of these exclusions apply when the item is faulty.

Orders that lapse before payment

An order that is never paid for is released automatically after 72 hours and the items go back into stock. Nothing is charged and there is nothing to return. We email you when that happens.
